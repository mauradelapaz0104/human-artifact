1. Back to the Origins

Technology has been in the works for over a century. Going back to the 1900s, we began to see more rapid, complex technologies emerge. The process of making technology back then was much more complicated than it is now because of their limited resources. Since these technologies were not made as easily as they are now, some of them were even considered a luxury at the time. It was very interesting to find out that the hand-held watch was one of these luxuries at some point. It sounds crazy to hear that at some point "knowing the time" was something not everyone had the privilege of. As time went on, there were new technologies that became available to the public.

2. Growth in a Century

For many years, the growth of technology was slow and steady, but after the Industrial Revolution, the growth became exponential. Along with the creation of new technological inventions there came many upgrades to existing technology, this is something we see to this day.

3. Age of Technology

In today's day and age, technology is seen/used in every corner. It has become an outlet that people cannot imagine living without. It is such a crucial part of their day-to-day lives that they see technology as a means of survival and not as something additional but not necessary. Technology is used in every outlet. As a college student, I witness this every day. I have to use technology and AI assistance to complete all of my coursework. I cannot imagine how I could do all of my work without having any technology, and as of right now, with how my classes are set up, not using technology is not an option.

Fun fact: There are roughly 1.8B AI users in the world, and 600 million of them use it on a daily basis. This is over one-eighth of the world's population.

4. When Does it Become a Serious Problem?

557%

As of right now, technology and AI usage and growth are on a steady rise. Once AI was able to grow big enough, its spread became inevitable because of how easily accessible it is. The problem arises when people begin using technology for something other than what it was designed for. AI was a tool designed to help people. Since not everyone thinks in an ethical manner, there are many people who use this technology in ways that will benefit them (mostly economically), even if this means it will hurt others. That big percentage is how much the artificial intelligence market is expected to grow by 2035. If this information proves to be accurate, this means that it will be increasing 556% in size from what it was in 2025.

One example of many that is seen with AI usage right now is the spread of misinformation. AI is currently being used to generate fake voices and influencers who are manipulated to spread misinformation, which is believed by the audience because they are made to look like credible individuals.

5. Foot on the Brake

After so many years of AI expansion, we have come to a point where expansion should no longer be our main concern, and gears need to be shifted towards how this uncontrollable expansion of AI can be controlled to some extent. There is no definitive solution to this problem yet because there are many limitations. Nevertheless, it is something that needs a lot of thought put into it.
